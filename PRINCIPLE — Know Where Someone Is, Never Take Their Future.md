# PRINCIPLE — Know Where Someone Is. Never Take Their Future.

**Ruled by Saba, 2026-07-28. Recorded verbatim the night he spoke it.**

---

## THE RULING, IN HIS WORDS

> **"One thing I'd like in the layer is a little bit of wisdom of understanding
> where somebody is, not to try to take over their future. The Lord loves free
> will, and I don't want to ever be guilty of stealing opportunities from
> someone."**

Spoken in the same breath as this, about his own life:

> *"I think I'm allowing God to take my life apart as a living seed for those who
> might want to consider it. And one of the it is is — consider what is this life,
> and how can I make it."*

He was reaching for **1 Corinthians 4:9** — the apostles set forth as a spectacle
to the world, to angels and to men. *Exhibited.* Set where everyone can see.

**Note for the record:** that verse is NOT in the archive. Searched — every
apparent hit is unrelated or a transcription artifact in the 1973 and 1977 tapes.
He has never preached it into this record. He said it tonight.

---

## WHAT IT BINDS

This is a constraint on **the system**, not only on the man. It governs every
layer that speaks to a person: search, the asking screens, any AI answering from
this archive, and any gate that ever opens outward.

**The system MAY:**
- show what the record holds about where a person is
- surface a spread of moments, and say plainly what each one is
- hold the door open and leave the light on
- say *the record does not show it* when it does not

**The system MAY NOT:**
- tell a person what to do next
- rank one path above another as the right one for them
- complete a discovery that a person could make themselves
- present a conclusion where evidence and a choice belong
- answer, on Saba's behalf, a question he would not answer himself

---

## HOW IT SITS WITH THE LAWS ALREADY STANDING

This is not a new fence. It is the **software form** of three fences already
built, and it closes the gap between them.

**Law 10 — Do not finish the book. Leave the game for other people.**
> *"If I do too much, I will take the game away from other people."*

James Doss found his own grandfather praying in 1989 **because it had not been
found yet.** A labelled moment would have handed him a fact instead of a
discovery. Tonight's ruling extends that from the naming work to everything the
system says to anyone.

**Law 13 — No mates, no dates, no states.**
Already scoped to *"his ministry, his counsel, and anything this archive ever
answers on his behalf."* Born from watching one genuine word become a queue of
hundreds asking to be told who to marry. **The machine inherits the fence.** If
Saba will not tell a person who to marry, when a thing will happen, or where to
live, then nothing built here may do it for him.

**Participation, not accomplishment.**
His credo. A system that decides for someone has accomplished something *at their
expense.* A system that shows them where they are has invited them in.

---

## THE TEST, FOR ANYONE BUILDING ON THIS

Before any answer leaves the system, one question:

> **Does this hand the person evidence, or does it hand them a verdict about
> their own life?**

Evidence may go out. A verdict about a person's future may not.

Where the two are hard to separate, the answer is **evidence plus the open
question** — never the resolved one.

### What the numbers show

**THREAT 41.9** — more than double his highest full year, **3.6× his 2021 level.**
The clearest outlier in the table.

**OUTWARD 158.4** — highest by a third. *They, them, the world.*

**REPENT 1.2** — his **lowest**. Against 1.7 in 2021, the highest in the record.

**REST 31.4** — his **highest**, above every full year. In the sermon carrying the
most threat language he was also reaching for rest harder than in any year measured.

**2021 is the calm end of the table** on every axis: least threat, least outward,
most repentance. It is also the year holding four recorded public self-corrections
— svc 571, 595, 637, 640.

### The confound, stated plainly

**Matthew 24 is an apocalyptic text.** Wars, deception, tribulation and false
prophets are in the passage itself. Any preacher on that chapter scores high on
THREAT regardless of his state. **Some portion of 41.9 belongs to Matthew, not to
Thomas.**

The sample is 8,587 words against years of a million. One sermon is a point, not
a trend.

**The test that would separate subject from speaker has NOT been run:** score
other preachers' Matthew 24 sermons in this archive, and score his own sermons on
other apocalyptic texts. Until that is done, the outlier is suggestive and not
established.

### What cannot be measured

**There is no attributed corpus for 2024, 2025 or 2026.** His attributed preaching
runs 2016–2021 only. *Where he is now* is not answerable from the book as it
stands. The table reaches 2021, plus one sermon in 2023, and stops.

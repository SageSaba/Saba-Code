## THE KEY, AS SABA RULES IT

Romans 8:28 — all things work together for good to them that love God, to them
who are the called according to his purpose.

**His amendment, in his own words, 2026-07-28:**

> **"I know if you did a ministry check on me, I would always change that,
> because I would say… they are the called if you respond to his purpose."**

The calling is not conferred and passive in his reading. **Response is the
condition.** This is consistent with every other law he has laid down —
participation, not accomplishment; freedom to obey; the mouth serves the house.

Everything below is to be read against that.

**What this bears on:**

- It stands directly behind the standing rule that he is **never** to be routed

**NOTHING IS TO BE ATTEMPTED HERE.** No recovery, no reset, no chasing the old

### HIS RULING ON HOW THIS SEASON MAY BE TOLD

Spoken 2026-07-28, deciding what of it may stand and what may not:

> **"It is personal because I could conjecture what this happened and that
> happened and that happened. Everybody does it. But they can learn from what God
> was doing in me, through me, by me — and yet watching me fall apart. I see it
> from my side, and I can't understand how they dealt with the pain. And I really
> love them."**

**The ruling, as it binds:**

| may stand | may not |
|---|---|
| what God was doing in him, through him, by him | conjecture about causes — *this happened and that happened* |
| that he fell apart, in his own words | any account of what it cost the people around him |
| his own side, named as his own side | any narration of another person's pain |

**This is the same fence he set once before**, from the opposite direction. On
Bishop Daquan Baldwin he kept WHAT HAPPENED and marked WHY as **UNKNOWN**,
withdrawing his own reading of another man's motive: *"it requires me to discern
what was in his heart, and I really don't know."* Here he applies it to himself:
his falling apart is testimony; the causes are conjecture; and what it did to
others is not his to tell.

**On Pastor James Doss**, spoken the same night:

> *"Everything he's ever said or done for me has been for my strength, but it was
> filtered through my heart. And I know it had to hurt. I'm sorry."*

Recorded at Saba's word, as his own account, of his own reception. **The apology
was not relayed and is not to be relayed** — it belongs in his voice.

**Two facts already in the record, from the same season:**
- svc 2522, in the low place: *"Pastor James and I were talking today. Pastor
  James, I'm probably not going to do that"* — then he stops himself and adds
  *"unless the Lord tells me different."*
- The same service: Saba's portion ends ~55:16. **James Doss stands up at 55:48
  and preaches the close** Saba did not reach.

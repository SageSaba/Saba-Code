## READ THIS FIRST — What This Evidence Can And Cannot Support

Four limits govern everything below. They are not caveats; they change what the
evidence is capable of showing.

**1. The corpus is mostly other men's preaching.**
2,247 services carry transcripts, 3,026,090 lines. Only **577,290 lines (19%)**
carry any speaker attribution, and only **12 names** were ever used. The bulk of
this archive is Loran Helm, Robert Morgan, Oliver Hogue, and the WOG/RFOD
gatherings — not Thomas Young. A theme appearing in 1976 is a witness that the
theme was **present in the rooms he sat in**, not that he preached it.

**2. Thomas Young is attributed only 2016–2021.**
Six years. Nothing before 2016 is credited to him. Where a line below is marked
**[ATTRIBUTED: Thomas Young]** it is his voice by the book's own record. Every
unmarked line is someone in the room — possibly him, possibly not. The book
cannot presently tell you which.

**3. Two requested themes return ZERO.**
- **"Omer" / "counting the Omer"** — 0 genuine occurrences. All 635 apparent
  matches are substrings of *overcomer*, *newcomer*, *conglomeration*. Word-boundary
  search returns nothing.
- **"Shavuot"** — 0 occurrences, all fifty years.
The Hebrew feast vocabulary now central to his work is **absent from the entire
preaching record.** This is the single largest gap found.

**4. Counts are deduplicated.**
32 services carry corrupted transcripts with lines repeated up to 3,210 times
(worst: svc 233 — 6,032 lines, 3 distinct). All figures below count DISTINCT
lines. Raw counts elsewhere in the system are inflated by this defect.
